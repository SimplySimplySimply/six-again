HOW TO PLAY

Hold your phone sideways. You attack up the field, and the whole width of the pitch fits on screen.

LEFT THUMB · RUN

Touch anywhere on the left and drag to steer. Push up to sprint, which burns stamina.

RIGHT THUMB · PASS

Swipe left or right. Short swipe hits the nearest runner. Long swipe throws a cut-out over one or two players.

PASSING SKILL

Good passers (high HAN) throw flat, fast balls that beat a rushing line and are hard to intercept. Poor passers loop it, giving defenders time to read it.

SHAPE

Tap SHAPE at the ruck. Your halfback is first receiver. Swipe sideways to hit the front-door forward, or sideways and down to go out the back. With room on the open side you get a second shape further out, and your back-door player can do it again.

TRICKS

Swipe against the way you're running for an inside ball. Swipe one way then back for a dummy. Swipe while being tackled to offload.

TAP · BEAT YOUR MAN

Tap the right side to side step, or to goose step when a defender comes across at you. Hold a direction on the left thumb to choose your step. Tap just as you're hit to break the tackle. Backs fend, forwards bump off.

KICK · SWIPE UP

Swipe forward to kick. Direction aims, length sets distance. A short swipe grubbers it along the ground. A longer swipe punts it, or bombs it inside their 35. Aim a punt through the posts for a field goal. The yellow marker shows the kick and where it lands. On a bomb, tap when TAP NOW! shows to send your chaser up for the ball. Good kickers land it close to the marker; poor kickers can shank it.

DEFENCE

Pick a line speed before each of their sets and watch it play out. Change it between tackles. Use ▶▶ to speed up. KILL hits hardest, but high tackles give away penalties and can get your players sin-binned or sent off.

DIFFICULTY

AMATEUR, PRO or ELITE change how fast and sharp the other team is. ADAPTIVE gets harder as you win and easier as you lose. Change it on the home screen or in the pause menu.

GOAL KICKS

Tap to lock your aim, then tap to set power. Watch the wind.

FIRST TIME?

The tutorial is a practice match against a training squad of five who don't defend hard. A coach pops up to show you each control: running, passing, stepping, passing from the ruck, kicking and defence. It takes about 3 minutes.
